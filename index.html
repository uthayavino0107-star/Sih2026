💧 What is JalNetra AI?

JalNetra ("water-eye") is a ₹5,800 solar-powered IoT node + AI brain that watches village water 24×7 in Jharkhand's mining belt. It estimates arsenic, lead, cadmium and fluoride without a laboratory, computes the BIS 10500 Water Quality Index, physically controls the purifier, locks the tap when water turns toxic, and SMS-alerts families in local languages. Built by Team AquaLens for Smart India Hackathon 2026 — problem statement SIH26040, Government of Jharkhand.

🧠 On-board AI (5 models)

Soft-Sensor Virtual Lab ★Potability 99.97%Contamination 8-class · 99.3%Anomaly WatchdogFilter-Life R² 0.95

Trained from scratch on 30,000 chemically-realistic samples grounded in BIS IS 10500:2012 limits and real district contamination profiles — Jharia AMD, Sahibganj arsenic, Gumla fluoride, Jamshedpur industrial.

🏛 Government pillars covered

① Distributed solar sensor nodes (pH, TDS, turbidity, level, soil-moisture, vibration · IP66 · GSM/LoRa) · ② multi-stage edge purification with offline rule engine · ③ cloud early-warning: plume map, forecasting, vernacular SMS · ④ affordability: ₹5,800 capex, ₹180/month opex, local-repair design.

📡 This web app

Runs in two modes: standalone demo (built-in realistic simulation — works offline, installable as a PWA) and server-live (auto-connects to the JalNetra FastAPI when hosted beside it). Instalable on any phone from the browser menu: "Add to Home Screen".
